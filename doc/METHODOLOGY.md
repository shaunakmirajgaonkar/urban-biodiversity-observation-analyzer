# Methodology

UrbanBioTrack is designed as a transparent screening tool rather than a population model. Its observation attention score combines habitat pressure, human disturbance, low observed abundance, observation confidence and a neutral seasonal term. Habitat intelligence combines native vegetation, canopy, water availability, seasonal stability and fragmentation/pressure indicators.

All scores are normalized to 0–100 and grouped into Low, Moderate, High and Critical bands. The calculations are deterministic and reproducible from the supplied local CSVs.
